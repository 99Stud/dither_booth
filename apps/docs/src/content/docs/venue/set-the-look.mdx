The event's **kit** sets how the booth screen and the tickets look: text, fonts, background, camera screen and tickets. Everything you change starts from the kit's own look, and only your changes are saved with the event.

Where: admin → **Event** → **Kit**.

## Change the look

<Steps>

1. **Pick the kit.** Choose it under **Kit**. Picking another kit starts the fonts, background and camera screen over from that kit; your text changes carry across.

2. **Change what you need**, card by card (below). The **Live preview** shows your unsaved changes. Pick a screen to preview: **Home screen**, **Camera**, **Photo receipt**, **Lottery win** or **Lottery loss**. Hold **Compare** to see the kit's own look, and **Replay intro** to watch the home screen come in again.

3. **Save** in the bar at the bottom. The booth screen updates straight away, with no reload. **Discard** drops everything since the last save.

</Steps>

Under **Kit**, the line under the picker says what differs from the kit. **Reset to kit defaults** drops every change at once.

## Typography

One font for each role: **Display**, **Body** and **Mono**. Each says where it's used. The undo button next to a font goes back to the kit's.

**Font library** adds a font to this booth: search **Google Fonts**, or **Upload woff2** (up to 2 MB). The booth keeps the font, so it works without Wi-Fi. A font an event uses can't be removed.

## Background

What moves behind the booth screen: **Fluid ascii** or **Charcoal smoke**.

- **Look**: a starting point, picked in one click. The first one is the kit's own look. Once you move a control, the look shows as **edited**.
- **Sliders** start in the middle, which is the kit's look; each end is worded (**Calm** to **Lively**, **Thin** to **Thick**…). The reset button next to a slider returns it to the kit's look.
- **Colours**: pick a swatch, or set each colour yourself.
- **Retro effects**: **Ascii screen**, **Scanlines**, **Film grain** and **CRT curve**, over the whole background.
- **Back to the kit's look** resets the background.

<Aside>
  Slider ends stay within limits that keep the iPad smooth. If the booth screen
  stutters, move **Flow speed**, **Trail** or **Smoke amount** back toward the
  middle.
</Aside>

## Camera screen

The old-monitor effect over the live camera and the frozen photo. The printed photo never has it, and it's separate from the background's retro effects.

- **CRT lines**: scanlines and a slow sweep.
- **Colour wash**: a light tint over the picture. **Match the background** follows the background's brightest colour; **Custom** lets you pick a **Tint colour**.
- **Strength**: how visible the lines and the wash are.

## Text

One card per screen or ticket: **Home screen**, **Visit prompts**, **Maintenance screen**, **Photo receipt** and **Lottery ticket**. Each line starts as the kit's text and shows **Changed** once you edit it; its undo button goes back to the kit's. Wrap words in `**` to make them bold. An optional line left empty is hidden; a required one can't be saved empty.
