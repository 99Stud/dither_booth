Studio's **Events** tab lists what each booth is booked for: **On now**, **Coming up** and **Past**. Platform admins book events; everyone in the client's team or the booth's team can read them.

An [event](/docs/start-here/glossary/) in Studio is the booking: the client, the booth and when. The booth's own event in admin (look, lottery, prizes) is still set up there, as before: [Run the event](/docs/venue/run-the-event/).

## Book an event

Platform admins only.

<Steps>

1. In **Events**, choose **Book an event**.

2. Fill in **Name**, the **Client** (the team it's for), the **Booth**, and when it **Starts** and **Ends**.

3. Choose **Book event**.

</Steps>

A new client needs its team first: in **Team**, choose **New team**.

If Studio says the booth **already has** another event then, pick another booth or time: a booth is at one event at a time. One can start when the last ends.

While an event is on, a booth that goes quiet for 3 minutes is a **Silent booth** alert, and one whose iPad shows no picture for 2 minutes (off, asleep, or the Shutter closed) is a **Camera off** alert.

## Pick who's on call

Before the night, choose who gets the booth's alerts.

<Steps>

1. In **Events**, open the event.

2. Under **On call**, switch on everyone who should get the alerts that night. They don't need to be on site.

3. Read the line under **On call**: it says who gets the alerts.

</Steps>

| It says                                                               | Meaning                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **They get Booth A's alerts during Halloween.**                       | Only the people switched on get them.                                              |
| **Nobody on call: the whole team gets Booth A's alerts.**             | Nobody is switched on, so everyone in the booth's team gets them.                  |
| **None of them has a device yet, so the whole team gets the alerts.** | The people on call never turned on notifications. Ask them to, in **This device**. |

Each person's line says whether their phone will ring: **1 device**, **No device yet: won't get alerts**, or **Hasn't signed in yet**. For someone on call, the last two turn amber: they're counted on and won't hear. Only platform admins change who's on call; everyone else sees the switches greyed out.

If you're the only one in the booth's team, Studio says so: choose **Open Team** and add people first.

You can change it during the night: someone switched on gets the next reminder of an alert already open.

## Check your phone will ring

If you're on call, open **This device** on your phone. It says **You're on call** for the event, with the booth and the time. If it also says **Turn on notifications**, choose **Turn on notifications** below it, then **Send test notification**.

## Change or cancel an event

Platform admins only. Open the event, then:

- **Change**: the name, the booth or the time. The client can't change: cancel and book it again.
- **Cancel event**, then **Cancel event** to confirm. Nobody stays on call for it.

Studio refuses to move an event to a booth of another team while people are on call for it: take them off call first.
