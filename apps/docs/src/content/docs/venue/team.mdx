Studio's **Team** tab shows who is in your [team](/docs/start-here/glossary/) and whether their phone will ring when a booth needs someone. Owners and platform admins can change it; everyone else can only read it.

## Read the list

The line under the team's name counts its members and how many of them get alerts. Under each address:

| It says                             | Meaning                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **2 devices**                       | Their phones get the team's alerts.                                             |
| **No device yet: won't get alerts** | They signed in, but never turned on notifications. Ask them to.                 |
| **Hasn't signed in yet**            | They were added but haven't opened Studio. Send them the sign-in message again. |

## Add someone

<Steps>

1. In **Team**, under **Add someone**, type their email address.

2. Choose **Member**, or **Owner** for someone who should also add and remove people. Choose **Add**.

3. Choose **Share** (or **Copy message**) and send them the message. It tells them to open Studio, sign in with that address and the code they're emailed, and turn on notifications in **This device**.

</Steps>

Nothing is emailed until they ask for their code. To send the message again, choose **⋯** on their row, then **Share sign-in**.

## Make someone an owner, or a member again

Choose **⋯** on their row, then **Make owner** or **Make member**.

## Take someone out of the team

Choose **⋯** on their row, then **Remove from team**, and **Remove** to confirm. Their phones stop getting the team's alerts at once and they no longer see its booths. They can still sign in, and you can add them again.

Nobody can remove themselves or stop being an owner themselves: ask another owner or a platform admin.

## Start a team

Platform admins only, for a new client or another 99Stud team. In **Team**, choose **New team**, type its name, and choose **Create team**. Studio switches to it: add its people next. A client's team sees the events booked for it in **Events**.
