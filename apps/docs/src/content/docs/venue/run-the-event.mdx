Admin is at `https://<booth>.local:3002` (or `https://<booth IP>:3002`). It has three pages: **Control Center**, **Event** and **Settings**, and two for tech staff at the sidebar's foot: **Logs** and **System**.

## The event

Book it in Studio first and pick who's on call for it: [Events and on call](/docs/venue/events/). The steps below set up the event on the booth itself.

The booth runs **one active event at a time**. An event has a name, a **kit** (how the booth screen and the tickets look: [Set the event's look](/docs/venue/set-the-look/)), the **ticket items** printed on the photo receipt, a **lottery** and its **prizes**.

- **Create event** when none is active.
- **Edit details**, next to the event's name at the top of the Event page, changes its name, when it **Starts** and when it **Ends**. Set both before the doors open: only guests who tap **Start** in between count in the Overview, so your setup tests and packing up don't. Pick each day from its calendar, then type the time. **Clear starts** or **Clear ends**, at the bottom of the calendar, leaves that side open. An event with neither counts every guest.
- **Replace event**, in the three-dot menu next to **Edit details**, for the next booking. The current event is **archived**, never deleted: its lottery, prizes, stock history and draws stay on record. Ticket numbers stay unique across all events.
- Changing the event's name, start, end or kit is an edit, not a replacement.

The Event page's **Overview** shows the night at a glance, updated every 10 s. From the top:

- Where the event stands, **Upcoming**, **On now** or **Ended** (once it has a start or an end), its start and end (or its first guest when it has neither), and one sentence on how it's going:
  - before the start, when it starts;
  - then **Busy right now** (5 guests or more in the last 15 minutes), **Going well**, or **Quiet for now** (no guest for 30 minutes, worth a look at the booth). Before the first guest it says **No guests yet**;
  - after the end, how many guests came.

  Under it, how many visits outside the start and end aren't counted.

- Four numbers, shown from before the first guest so nothing moves when guests arrive:
  - **Guests**: every guest of the event so far between its start and end, and how many came in the last 15 minutes. A guest counts from their **Start** tap, so one who walks away mid-visit still counts.
  - **Photos printed**, with the share of guests that got one.
  - **Prizes won**, once the lottery has drawn, and how many guests drew for each win ("1 in 3 guests won").
  - **Time at the booth**: the average from **Start** to the receipt, as "1 min 20 s".
- How guests went through: **Started**, **Took a photo**, **Got their ticket**, and **Walked away** (guests who left before the end).
- **Guests per hour** from the event's start (or its first guest) to now, or to its end, one bar per hour up to the last 24, with **Busiest** and its count above. The hour still running is the lighter bar, labelled **Now**; after the end, no bar is. Touch a bar for its count.
- **Needs a look**: what to act on now, one row each with its button:
  - a ticket that didn't print, with its ticket number and time. **Look up ticket** opens **Draws** on that ticket, to find the guest and see if they won. Without a number, **Control center**;
  - a booth problem: paper running low or out, printer cover, booth screen, camera, or tickets that can't be prepared (**Control center**);
  - a prize that ran out, has 1 left, or could run out within the hour at tonight's pace (**Restock**). Only while the lottery is live;
  - an event with no start or end yet (**Set start and end**).

  When there's nothing, it says the screen, camera and printer are fine.

- **Lottery**: **Off** says guests get their photo with no prize draw, with **Turn on** (or **Add prizes** when there are none). **Live** lists the prizes left by rarity, until when they should last at this pace and, during a pause after a win, when the next win can come. During the first hour the pace is your estimate from the Lottery tab; after that it's the booth's own over the hours on the chart. **Lottery settings** opens the Lottery tab.

## The lottery

Every guest visit runs one draw while the lottery is live. The guest sees the result on the slot machine, then gets a ticket.

With the lottery off, guests see no lottery at all: the landing screen has no prize panel at the top right, and after the photo there is no slot machine. The photo stays in the middle of the screen while the receipt prints. The booth screen follows the switch straight away, with no reload. A guest already in a visit finishes it: if their draw ran against the lottery, the slot machine shows the result, and if you switched it off after their reels appeared, the reels land on “no prize”.

| Setting                   | Meaning                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lottery is live / off** | Off: no prize is ever drawn, and guests see no lottery. Visits still print the photo receipt.                             |
| **No-win weight**         | The weight of “no prize” in the draw. Higher means fewer winners. New events start at 50.                                 |
| **Pause after a win**     | Minutes after a win during which every draw is a loss. Stops a run of winners in a row. `0` lets guests win back to back. |
| **Print loser ticket**    | Also print a ticket when the guest doesn't win.                                                                           |

### How the odds work

Each prize **with stock left** has a **weight**; by default it comes from its rarity:

| Rarity    | Default weight |
| --------- | -------------- |
| Common    | 40             |
| Uncommon  | 20             |
| Rare      | 8              |
| Epic      | 4              |
| Legendary | 2              |

A draw picks one outcome with probability **weight ÷ (sum of all weights + no-win weight)**. With the default no-win weight and only a common prize, a guest wins about 1 in 2.3; with only a legendary prize, about 1 in 26. **The more prizes in the draw, the more often someone wins**; a prize that runs out leaves the draw and the others' odds go up. The Event page's odds and forecast cards compute this for you — check them after every change.

<Aside type="caution">
  Weights don't depend on how much stock is left: a prize with 1 unit left is as
  likely as one with 100, until it runs out. If you want a prize to last the
  whole event, give it a lower weight, not just a lower stock.
</Aside>

## Prizes and stock

A prize has a **title**, a **rarity**, a **weight** (optional, overrides the rarity default), and **How to claim it** — the instruction printed on the winning ticket, such as “Show this ticket at the bar”.

Stock is a ledger, not a number you overwrite:

- **Add prize** with **How many you have** creates the initial stock.
- **Restock** adds units (or removes them with a negative number). Each restock is applied exactly once, even if the network hiccups and the form retries.
- Each win takes one unit at the moment of the draw. The booth refuses a win that would take stock below zero.
- **Delete** removes a prize from future draws; draws that already won it keep its title.

You can restock during the event at any time, without closing the booth.

When the lottery is live and every prize is out, every guest loses: the phones that get the booth's alerts are warned (**Lottery empty**): those of the staff on call during an [event](/docs/venue/events/), otherwise the whole team's. Restock a prize, or switch the lottery off, and it clears by itself.

## Draws and ticket lookup

The **Draws** table lists every draw of the active event, wins and losses, newest first. Visits while the lottery was off aren't draws, so they aren't listed. To check a ticket a guest brings back, type its six digits (or scan its `STUD_DITHERBOOTH_XXXXXX` code) into the lookup: it shows the draw that ticket belongs to, **No lottery** for a visit while the lottery was off, or “No visit printed this ticket number.” Test prints and sample lottery tickets use `000000` and never draw.

## Control Center

The page to keep open on your phone during the event.

| Card              | What it shows and does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Booth screen**  | **Photo booth / Darkroom**: switch what the iPad shows. **Maintenance**: close the booth to guests. **Automatic**: let the printer close and reopen the booth on its own.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Printer**       | Live printer state (ready, paper low, out of paper, cover open, error, not connected), since when, whether it can print, and the last changes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Booth camera**  | **Watch live**: see what the iPad camera sees, to check framing from across the room. Stops after 10 minutes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Health checks** | Engine, Shutter, Camera, Printer, the ticket renderer (Puppeteer) and the Pi itself (temperature, throttling, memory, storage). Each item explains what is wrong. From here you can reload the kiosk page (now, or when the current guest is done), re-initialize Puppeteer, or restart a service. **View logs** opens that item's logs; the Pi's **View system** opens the [System page](/docs/maintenance/system/). |
| **Network**       | The booth's network and address, the QR and link to connect a device, and **Print setup ticket**.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

### Maintenance

Switching **Maintenance** on shows guests a “be right back” screen. A guest in the middle of a visit finishes first; the screen switches once the iPad is back on the landing screen (at most about 90 seconds later). The Darkroom keeps working.

The booth is in maintenance while **any** reason holds:

- **Manual** — you switched it on. Only you switch it off; a fixed printer never reopens the booth.
- **Printer** — the printer can't print (out of paper, cover open, error, unplugged), with **Automatic** on. It clears on its own once the printer has been ready for 3 seconds.

If you switch maintenance off while the printer is still the reason, the card says so and the booth stays closed until the printer is fixed.

**Automatic** should stay on. Turn it off only if a misbehaving sensor keeps closing the booth for no reason: then the booth only refuses to print when the printer is not connected at all.

## Settings

- **Wi-Fi** — save, forget and switch networks; run on the booth Wi-Fi. See [Wi-Fi at a venue](/docs/venue/wifi/).
- **Printing** — which end of the photo and lottery tickets comes out of the printer first: **Bottom first** (the default) or **Top first**. **Print a sample ticket** prints a sample lottery ticket to check, without using a prize.
- **Hub** — type a [registration code](/docs/start-here/glossary/) from the Studio app and choose **Register**, so the booth's alerts reach staff phones. **Forget the registration** stops that.
- **Darkroom PIN** — 4 to 8 digits. Changing it signs out every open Darkroom.
- **Appearance** — light or dark theme for admin itself.
- **Docs** (on a phone) — opens this documentation. The booth serves it itself at `/docs/` on the admin address, so it works at a venue with no internet. On an iPad or a computer, it's the sidebar's **Docs** link.
- **Logs** and **System** (on a phone) — open the [Logs page](/docs/maintenance/logs/#open-logs) and the [System page](/docs/maintenance/system/). On an iPad or a computer, they are the sidebar's **Logs** and **System** links.
